Synthetic demo data: suppliers, properties, prices, availability and reliability are fictional. No bookings are made.

Comparable, not flattened.

SupplyLens groups the same hotel or experience across suppliers, keeps the room, transfer, meal and policy differences that matter, and recommends the best eligible offer for your constraints, with the reasons shown.

Try: marina hotel with breakfast, free cancellation · beach stay with pool · evening activity with hotel pickup · observation deck ticket

    What suppliers send.

    The same inventory as raw feeds deliver it: every supplier record listed separately. This is the baseline SupplyLens consolidates.

    Uncertain stays visible.

    Pairs with mixed evidence are never merged automatically. A reviewer decides; every decision is logged and can be reversed.
